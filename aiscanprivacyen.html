1-Second AI Scanner Privacy Policy

Effective Date: July 12, 2026

ReviewTube Inc. ("we," "us," or "the Developer") values the privacy of users of "1-Second AI Scanner" (the "App") and complies with applicable data protection laws, including South Korea's Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App handles and what rights you have.

1. Information We Collect and Do Not Collect

The App does not require sign-up or login. To distinguish users and manage daily usage limits, the App automatically assigns a random identifier to your device using Firebase's "Anonymous Authentication" feature. This identifier is not linked to your name, email, or any other real-world identity, and cannot by itself identify a specific person.

  • Not collected: name, phone number, email address, nickname, location data, payment information
  • Automatically assigned: an anonymous identifier (per device/install, not personally identifiable)
  • May be automatically collected: advertising identifiers and other device information — see Section 5

2. Camera Permission and Scanned Document Images — Sent for AI Analysis

The App requests camera access for its core feature: document scanning and AI analysis.

Unlike our previous app ("1-Second Scanner"), this App sends the document image you capture or select from your gallery to Google's AI servers (Gemini, via Firebase AI Logic) in order to process the feature you requested (3-line summary, translation, table-to-CSV, or simplified explanation).

The transmitted image is used only momentarily to generate the AI response and is never sent to or stored on the Developer's own servers. The AI-generated response (summary, translation, table data, etc.) is likewise never stored on the Developer's servers — it is shown only on your screen.

The image and the AI response are cleared from your device's memory once you process a new document or close the App; they are not separately saved to your photo gallery.

This App accesses the Gemini API through a Google Cloud project on a paid (billed) tier. Under Google's terms for paid services, submitted images, prompts, and responses are not used to improve Google's own products or to train AI models; they are only logged temporarily for safety purposes such as abuse prevention. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Gemini API Additional Terms of Service (Paid Services section).

3. Usage Information Recorded on Our Servers (Daily Limit & Feedback)

To operate the service, the App records only the numeric/categorical information below on the Developer's server (Google Firebase). Document content or AI response content is never included, under any circumstances.

  • Daily free-use tracking: the anonymous identifier from Section 1, today's usage count, bonus uses earned from watching reward ads, and the reference date
  • Feature feedback (👍/👎): which feature was rated (summary/translate/CSV/simplify), whether it was a thumbs-up or thumbs-down, your device's language setting, the time of the click, and the anonymous identifier from Section 1

This information is managed solely through the anonymous identifier, never a real name, and is used only to manage daily usage limits and to improve the App (for example, identifying whether a particular language's translations receive more negative feedback).

4. Purposes of Use

The information collected is used solely for the following purposes:

  • Providing the document analysis results you requested (summary, translation, table extraction, simplified explanation)
  • Managing your daily free-use limit and reward-ad bonus uses
  • Understanding per-feature satisfaction and improving the App
  • Displaying ads and personalized advertising
  • Operating the App reliably and diagnosing errors

5. Advertising and AI Service Providers (Third-Party Disclosure)

The App uses the following Google services:

  • Google AdMob: serves banner and rewarded ads. Advertising identifiers and device information may be automatically shared with Google.
  • Firebase AI Logic (Gemini): processes the AI analysis of the document image you submit (see Section 2).
  • Firebase Analytics: analyzes app usage statistics (no personally identifying information included).

Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. See the Google Privacy Policy for details. You can reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized ads from your device settings.

6. Data Retention Period

The App does not retain any membership information. Document images and AI responses are discarded immediately after processing and are never stored (see Section 2). Usage and feedback records (Section 3) are retained under the anonymous identifier for as long as the service operates; deleting the App does not by itself identify or remove any specific individual's past server-side records, since they were never tied to a real identity. Automatically collected data such as advertising identifiers is handled per Google's policies.

7. Your Rights

  • Revoke camera permission from your device settings (this will limit the scanning feature)
  • Reset your advertising identifier or opt out of personalized ads from your device settings
  • Delete on-device data by uninstalling the App (document images and AI responses are never stored on-device to begin with)
  • Request deletion of your server-side usage/feedback records — contact us using the information in Section 10

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under the age of 14 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes in applicable law, our policies, or the App's features. We will notify users of material changes via an in-app notice or by updating this page.
